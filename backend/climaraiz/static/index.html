How the application works

Rainfall
Sum of 24 hourly rates multiplied by one hour. Days with missing hours remain incomplete. Annual totals require every day.
Temperature
Mean of complete daily means. Annual means weight every day equally; daily maximum and minimum are not provided.
Calendar
Daily rainfall intervals are UTC 00:00–24:00. The interface does not silently relabel them as local civil days.
Trends
Theil–Sen slope with 95% confidence interval, original Mann–Kendall test, and approximate Ljung–Box diagnostics on detrended annual residuals.
Planting windows
User-supplied dates described as collected from ZARC. Soil, cultivar group, publication version, and validity have not been independently verified.
Scenario criteria matching
Percent of common complete historical years meeting every included criterion. Whole-cycle rain benchmarks: corn 500 mm, black beans 300 mm, sunflower 500 mm. Flowering dry spells: at least five days below 1 mm/day. Mean-temperature thresholds are exploratory. Required windows must be supplied; no success probabilities or risk bands are inferred.
Crop phases & risk
Optional phases are user-defined scenarios. Agricultural risk, favorable frequency, and a best planting date remain unavailable until agronomic criteria are validated.
